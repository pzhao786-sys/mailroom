# About the office

## Hours
Monday to Thursday 7:30 am to 5:00 pm, Friday 7:30 am to 1:00 pm. Closed weekends and on Thanksgiving Day and the day after. Front desk phone: (608) 555-0186.

## Team
Dr. Amara Okafor, DDS (restorative and cosmetic dentistry), Dr. Daniel Reyes, DMD (general and emergency dentistry), and Kelsey Moore, RDH (hygienist, cleanings).

## Patients we see
We see patients of all ages from 3 years old. Children's cleanings are scheduled with our hygienist; a parent or guardian must attend for patients under 18.

## Emergencies
We keep an emergency slot every weekday morning at 8:00 am. If you have severe pain, swelling, bleeding that will not stop, or a broken tooth, call the office right away. After hours, call the main number and follow the prompts for the on-call dentist.
