# Insurance and payments

## In-network plans
We are in network with Delta Dental PPO, Cigna Dental PPO, MetLife PDP and Guardian Dental. We do not accept Medicaid or BadgerCare dental plans. For other PPO plans we file claims as an out-of-network provider; the patient pays any difference.

## Benefits checks
Coverage depends on each patient's plan. The front desk will run a free benefits check before treatment if you send your plan name; we never guess coverage by email.

## Payment methods
We accept cash, checks, all major credit cards, and HSA and FSA cards. Payment is due at the time of service for any patient portion.

## Payment plans
For treatment over $500 we offer an in-house payment plan: 0% interest for 6 months, with 25% due at the first visit. Ask the front desk to set one up.

## Statements
Statements are mailed and emailed monthly. If you believe a statement is wrong, our billing coordinator will review your insurance explanation of benefits with you.
