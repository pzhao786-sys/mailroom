# Office policies

## Cancellations and rescheduling
Please give at least 24 hours' notice to cancel or reschedule. Appointments cancelled with less than 24 hours' notice, or missed, may be charged a $50 fee after the first occurrence.

## New patients
New patients receive intake forms by email after booking. Please complete them before the visit and arrive 10 minutes early. Bring your insurance card and a photo ID.

## Records requests
Copies of records are sent to another provider only with a signed release form from the patient or guardian. Requests are handled by the office manager within 5 business days.

## Privacy
We do not discuss one patient's appointments or treatment with anyone else, including family members, without the patient's written permission.
