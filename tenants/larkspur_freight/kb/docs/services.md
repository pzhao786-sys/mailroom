# Services and coverage

## Service area
Larkspur Freight picks up and delivers in 36 states plus DC across four regions: Midwest (OH, IN, MI, IL, WI, MN, IA, MO, KY), Northeast (PA, NY, NJ, CT, MA, RI, VT, NH, ME, MD, DE, DC), Southeast (WV, VA, NC, SC, GA, FL, TN, AL, MS) and South Central (TX, OK, AR, LA, KS, NE). We do not serve the Mountain or Pacific states directly; a team member can arrange partner coverage on request.

## Service levels
Standard LTL delivers in 2-5 business days depending on the lane. Expedited delivers in 1-2 business days and costs 40% more than standard linehaul. Transit times start the business day after pickup.

## Weight limits
LTL shipments are accepted up to 15,000 lbs. Heavier freight moves as full truckload (FTL), which our sales team quotes individually.

## Accessorial services
Liftgate at pickup or delivery: $85. Residential delivery: $110. Inside delivery: $140. Delivery appointment: $45. Accessorials must be requested when booking.

## Fuel surcharge
All quotes include a fuel surcharge, currently 22.5% of linehaul, reviewed monthly.

## Hazardous materials
We carry most hazmat classes with certified drivers, but every hazmat shipment is quoted and booked by a person so placarding and paperwork can be checked.

## Quotes
Quotes are valid for 14 days from the date issued and assume accurate weight and dimensions. Reweighs that find a higher weight are billed at the quoted rate per pound.

## Pickups
Pickup requests received by 2:00 pm ET are scheduled for the same day when a driver is available, otherwise the next business day. Customer service hours are Monday to Friday, 7:00 am to 7:00 pm ET.
