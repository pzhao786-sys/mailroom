# Freight claims policy

## Reporting damage
Visible damage or shortage should be noted on the delivery receipt before signing. Concealed damage (found after unpacking) must be reported within 5 business days of delivery.

## Filing deadline
Claims for loss, damage or shortage must be filed in writing within 9 months of the delivery date, or for lost freight, within 9 months of the scheduled delivery date.

## Documents needed
To process a claim we need: the bill of lading, the signed delivery receipt, photos of the damaged freight and packaging, a commercial invoice or proof of value for the goods, and a repair estimate if the goods can be repaired. Keep the damaged freight and packaging until the claim is closed; we may inspect it.

## Liability
Unless a higher value was declared and paid for at booking, our liability is limited to $25 per pound of the damaged or lost freight, up to $100,000 per shipment.

## Timeline
We acknowledge claims within 3 business days and resolve most claims within 30 days. Complex claims are resolved within 120 days as required by federal regulation.
