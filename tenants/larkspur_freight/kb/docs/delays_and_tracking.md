# Delays and tracking

## Tracking
Every shipment has a tracking number: LF followed by 8 digits, printed on the bill of lading. Customers can also reference their own PO number. Status updates happen at every terminal scan.

## Delays
Weather, road closures and terminal congestion can delay freight. When a shipment misses its ETA, we update the ETA within 4 business hours and notify the shipper. Delays alone are not eligible for compensation, but repeated service failures on a lane are reviewed by the operations manager.

## Proof of delivery
Proof of delivery (signed delivery receipt) is available the business day after delivery and can be emailed on request.

## Missed pickups
If a scheduled pickup is missed, dispatch reschedules it for the next available window, at no charge.
