# Billing and payments

## Customer invoices
Approved accounts pay on net-30 terms. Invoices are emailed the day after delivery. Billing disputes must be raised within 30 days of the invoice date; our billing team reviews each dispute and replies within 5 business days. Accessorial charges are billed only when requested at booking or performed at the customer's request.

## Vendor invoices (accounts payable)
Vendors send invoices to ap@larkspurfreight.example or ops@larkspurfreight.example. Purchases over $500 require a Larkspur purchase order (PO- followed by 5 digits). Utilities, rent and contract leases are exempt from the PO requirement. Approved vendor invoices are paid on net-30 terms in payment runs on Tuesdays and Fridays.

## Changes to bank details
Larkspur never changes a vendor's bank or remittance details based on an email or an invoice. Any change is verified by a phone call to the number already on file, made by our accounts payable team.
